## Template Usage Notes

**This template is for AI execution, not stakeholder approval.**

Keep it:
- **Concise:** One page is ideal, two pages max
- **Specific:** "Busy founders" → "Solo founders who take 5+ meetings/day"
- **Actionable:** Every section should guide implementation
- **Scoped:** Non-goals are as important as goals

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- ❌ Vague users ("anyone who wants to be productive")
- ❌ Feature lists without rationale
- ❌ Missing non-goals (leads to scope creep)
- ❌ Tech stack without reasoning
- ❌ Success criteria that can't be measured

**Good Examples:**

**Vague:** "A tool for developers"
**Specific:** "Frontend developers who manually test responsive designs across 5+ breakpoints"

**Vague:** "Fast search"
**Specific:** "Find any note in under 5 seconds using fuzzy search"

**Vague:** "User-friendly interface"
**Specific:** "Single-page layout with keyboard shortcuts for power users"
